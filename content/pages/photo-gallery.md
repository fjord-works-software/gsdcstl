## 2013 GSDC of St. Louis Annual Awards Ceremony and Dinner

*Held on February 21, 2014*

Award presentations to members and their dogs, including Royal Hecht Bred By Exhibitor, Select Excellent, CGC/ORT/TDI, Grand Champion, Champion, Best Puppy, Novice Obedience, Junior Herding Dog, Herding PreTrial, and Register of Merit awards.
