## Point System

**Class of 6+ entries:**

- 1st place — 5 points
- 2nd place — 4 points
- 3rd place — 3 points
- 4th place — 2 points

**Class of 2 entries:**

- 1st place — 1 point
- 2nd place — 0 points

Competition required for points to count.

## Special Awards

- GSDC of St. Louis Novice Obedience Award
- GSDC of St. Louis Advanced Obedience Award
- GSDC of St. Louis Dual Achievement Award
- GSDC of St. Louis Junior Handler Award
- GSDC of St. Louis Service Dog Breeder Award
