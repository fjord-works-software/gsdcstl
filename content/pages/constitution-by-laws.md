## Article I — Name and Objects

The club is named **The German Shepherd Dog Club of St. Louis, Inc.** Its objects are to encourage quality breeding; protect breed interests; demonstrate the versatility of the German Shepherd Dog; conduct shows and trials; and publish literature advancing the breed.

## Article II — Membership

Five membership types: **Regular, Household, Lifetime, Associate,** and **Junior** (ages 9–17).

- Regular, Household, and Lifetime members have voting privileges.
- Associate members have no voting rights.
- Junior members have waived dues and no voting rights.

**Annual dues:** Associate $15 · Single $30 · Household $50 (payable by January 1).

Applications require submission in person with two non-related sponsors and a 2/3 affirmative vote.

## Article III — Meetings and Voting

- Club meetings: at least 6 times per year; quorum of 20% required.
- Board meetings: at least 6 times per year.
- No proxy or absentee voting permitted.

## Article IV — Governors and Officers

The Board consists of 7 members: 5 elected governors plus the President and 4 officers. Officers serve 2-year terms. No more than 2 unexcused absences are permitted.

**Officers:** President · Vice-President · Recording Secretary · Treasurer · Corresponding Secretary

## Article V — Club Year, Annual Meeting, Elections

- Fiscal year: January 1 – December 31.
- Elections held by secret ballot in January.
- Nominating committee selected in September.

## Articles VI–XI

Cover committees, discipline procedures, amendment processes, and parliamentary authority (Robert's Rules of Order).
