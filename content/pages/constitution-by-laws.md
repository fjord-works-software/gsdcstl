## Article I — Name and Objects

The club is named **The German Shepherd Dog Club of St. Louis**. Its objects are to encourage quality breeding to the AKC-approved breed standard; protect and advance the breed by encouraging sportsmanlike conduct; demonstrate the versatility of the German Shepherd Dog; conduct shows, obedience trials and other AKC events; and publish literature in the interest of the breed. The Club is not operated for profit.

## Article II — Membership

Five membership types: **Regular, Household, Lifetime, Associate,** and **Junior** (ages 9–17).

- Regular, Household (each member over 18), and Lifetime members may vote and hold office.
- Lifetime membership requires at least 20 consecutive years of membership and Board approval; no dues.
- Associate members may not vote or hold office.
- Junior members pay no dues, may not vote or hold office, and may convert to regular membership at 18.

**Dues** may not exceed $15 (Associate), $30 (Single), or $50 (Household) per year, and are payable on or before January 1. Membership lapses if dues remain unpaid after January 31.

**Applications** must be submitted in person to the Recording Secretary at a monthly meeting with two non-related sponsors. The application is read at two meetings and voted on at the second by secret ballot; a two-thirds affirmative vote is required.

## Article III — Meetings and Voting

- Club meetings: at least six times a year, with ten days' written or electronic notice; quorum is 20% of members in good standing. Members may attend remotely, and remote attendance counts toward quorum.
- Board meetings: at least six times a year, in person or electronically; quorum is a majority of the Board.
- No proxy or absentee voting. Secret-ballot votes must be in person.

## Article IV — Governors and Officers

The **Board of Governors** consists of three selected Governors plus the President, Vice-President, Recording Secretary, Treasurer, Corresponding Secretary, and the immediate Past President. All terms are two years. Officers or Governors with more than two unexcused absences in a term are not eligible for a successive term.

**Officers:** President · Vice-President · Recording Secretary · Treasurer · Corresponding Secretary

## Article V — Club Year, Annual Meeting, Elections

- Fiscal year: January 1 – December 31.
- The Annual Meeting and elections (by secret, written ballot) are held in January.
- The Board selects a nominating committee in September. Additional nominations may be made **only from the floor at the November meeting**, and the nominee must be present to accept.
- Elections alternate years: President, Recording Secretary and Corresponding Secretary in odd years; Vice-President, Treasurer and Board positions in even years.

## Articles VI–XI

Cover committees, discipline (charges, Board hearings, and expulsion), amendments (two-thirds secret vote), the order of business, dissolution, and parliamentary authority (*Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised*).
